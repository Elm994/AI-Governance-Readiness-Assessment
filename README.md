# AI-Governance-Readiness-Assessment
Capstone Exercise (AJ Yawn, LinkedIn Learning) 

AI Risk and Regulation Essentials for GRC Engineers with AJ Yawn
https://www.linkedin.com/learning/ai-risk-and-regulation-essentials-for-grc-engineers

A practical AI governance readiness assessment of a fictional mid-sized global organisation using artificial intelligence across customer support, hiring, marketing, and fraud detection.

The assessment evaluates the organisation against:

ISO/IEC 42001 – AI Management System
NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
EU AI Act
A consolidated 90-day AI governance improvement plan

Disclaimer: This is a fictional worked example created as a portfolio/capstone exercise. It is not a formal certification audit, legal opinion, or compliance assessment.
